Looper

Looper has closed.

Looper was a golf matchup prediction platform. Across the 2025 and 2026 PGA Tour seasons it rated head-to-head and 3-ball matchups using strokes-gained data and a machine learning model built from the ground up.

Before deciding whether to keep going, we did the hard part: we measured the model against the betting market, correctly. The answer was clear. The market already prices everything the model knew. There was no edge to offer.

Rather than sell picks we could not stand behind, we shut it down. The site, the app, and all subscriptions are permanently closed. No further charges will be made.

Thanks to everyone who followed along.
